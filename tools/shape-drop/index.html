Shapes multiply inside the frame, fall with gravity and never overlap. Set the sequence of copies, choose the frame and colors, then export a seamless loop as video, animated HTML or Lottie. Upload your own PNG or SVG with a transparent background to use another shape.

Shape
Default shape: 6-point star. Upload a PNG or SVG with a transparent background to use its silhouette for collisions. Pick several files to make a group of pieces that fall and collide separately (they are re-arranged into a compact group so the frame fills up) (reload the page to go back to the star).
Sequence
At the start of each step all copies appear at once in the tightest layout, then gravity settles them. At the end everything disappears at once, so it loops cleanly. Up to 100 copies per step; 60 or fewer keeps the motion smooth.
