Fonts used by the Text Reveal tool (latin subset, WOFF), taken from the Fontsource packages.
Montserrat, Playfair Display, Oswald, Bebas Neue and Space Mono are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
Each font keeps the copyright of its authors. They are bundled so the tool can read the letter outlines and works without Google Fonts.
